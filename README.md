# Exploiting Underpriced Prop-Firm Evaluations

An independent quantitative research paper by Michael Nguyen (Bachelor of Advanced Finance and Economics (Honours), The University of Queensland).

**Read it here:** the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

Retail prop firms sell trading evaluations: the buyer pays a fee, trades a simulated account and is paid a share of later profit in real cash once a profit target is reached. The paper values these evaluations as double-barrier digital options on simulated profit, finds that Vest Capital's $10,000 evaluation is worth about $180 against an $80 fee for a trader with no skill, and captures the gap with a delta hedge on Hyperliquid sized to the account's real exposure. On three months of real prices with measured costs, plus a holdout recorded after the strategy was fixed, the hedged strategy returns about 11% a month on capital up to $150,000, capped near $24,000 a month by Vest's ten-account limit.
